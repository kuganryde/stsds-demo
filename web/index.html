What to look for

  1. Each document has a confidence score. 0.95 or more runs on its own.
  2. Anything lower stops for a person, and every human edit forces a second approver.
  3. Every filing is checked against the official calculation to the sen before it is paid.
  4. After stamping, nine final checks run before the case is marked complete.
  5. In the console, open Dashboard for compliance rate, alerts, penalty exposure (estimate) and the monthly review, and Contract register to search, add or import contracts. The register includes fictional sample contracts that are overdue, due soon or expiring.
